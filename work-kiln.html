Case study · Food

Kiln & Kettle. Origin story, then the order form.

Roastery site with a short origin story and a wholesale form. Built in a weekend. Still taking orders.

They had a good roast and a site that opened with a looping video of beans. Wholesale buyers bounced before they found the bag sizes. The owner was answering the same three questions by text.

The brief

One origin paragraph. Then the beans. Then the form. No slider. No fake scarcity. Weekend delivery because they had a market the following Saturday.

What shipped

A dark kiln palette, a short farm-to-roast line, and a wholesale form that asks for account name, bag size, and when they need it. Retail can wait. The Saturday market could not.

Tell the story once. Then get out of the way of the order.

Same chair. Different cloth. Still taking orders.
