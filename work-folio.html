Case study · Retail

North Folio. A shop that reads like a room.

Independent bookshop. Events calendar, staff picks, and a quiet palette that survives fluorescent hours.

The previous site treated the store like a catalog. Covers stacked, no voice, a newsletter form that dumped into a spreadsheet nobody opened. Readings were announced on a paper taped to the door.

The brief

Make the web feel like the reading room: paper, lamp, and a short list of books someone actually finished. Keep inventory on their existing system. Give events a page that can be updated without a developer.

What shipped

A locked template for shop, events, and staff picks. Type set for long sitting. Gold used as a bookmark, not a banner. The calendar is a simple list — date, title, who is reading — because a widget that looks like Ticketmaster does not belong next to poetry.

Staff picks first. Commerce second. The room still has to be a room.

Same chair as Harbor House. Different cloth.
