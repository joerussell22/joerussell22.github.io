Case study · Hospitality

Harbor House needed a site that felt like the inn.

A twelve-room mountain inn. The old site was a leftover with three booking buttons and none of them worked on a phone.

Guests found Harbor House on a map, then lost it in a carousel of stock lakes and a form that asked for a fax number. Staff were taking reservations on paper because the engine never made it past the homepage.

The brief

Keep the existing booking engine. Lose the chain-hotel look. Make every room page readable on a porch at dusk. Eight child pages, one locked nav, one gold-on-navy skin.

What shipped

We rebuilt on this scaffold. Room pages share a template. The Book button is a single, honest link to their engine — no third party overlay, no fake calendar. Copy names the creek, the woodstove, and the walk to town. Photos sit under a dark wash so type still holds.

A site that feels like the front desk, not like a franchise splash page.

Scope stayed written. Extra season pages wait for a new quote.
